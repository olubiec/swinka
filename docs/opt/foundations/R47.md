## 3. Room 256x144 (Architecture & Layout Evaluation)

Rooms default to 192×112 px (12×7 cells of 16 px, `DEFAULT_COLS = 12`, `DEFAULT_ROWS = 7`, `DEFAULT_WALL_ROWS = 2` in `web/home/features/places/room-geometry.js:6-8`, `src/Cuy.Core/World/SharedRoomOps.cs:35`, `src/Cuy.Core/GameState.PigRooms.cs:10`). `place_scenes.json:14` marks room with `size_from_layout: true`. Scene switching resizes canvas (`canvas-pig.js:778`), violating the brief rule: *"przy przełączaniu miejsc okno nie zmienia rozmiaru nawet o 1 px, bez pasków po bokach"*.

### Evaluation:
- **(a) Enlarge logical room grid to 16×9 cells (256×144 px):**
  If WallRows changes from 2 to 3 (48 px wall), saved items at `Y=2` violate `RoomLayoutOps.cs:65-76` (`CanPlace` enforces wall items on wall rows and floor items on floor rows). Also Salon upgrades (`assets/data/places.json:16-36`: 12×7 -> 16×8 -> 20×9) and Pig Rooms (`assets/data/pig_rooms.json:13-28`: 10×6 -> 12×7 -> 14×8) have levels smaller than 16×9. Enforcing 16×9 as base breaks progression levels.
- **(b) Keep logical room size and give stage fixed 16:9 frame:**
  Viewport canvas in `canvas-pig.js` is fixed to 256×144 px. Rooms smaller than 256×144 are displayed within the 256×144 stage framed by continuous wallpaper/skirting.
- **(c) Recommendation (Hybrid Visual Frame):**
  Fix canvas to 256×144 px in `web/home/canvas-pig.js:347,778`. In `web/home/features/places/room-geometry.js` and `scenes.js`, allow room background/flooring to tile across 256×144 (16×9 cells), while logical placement bounds follow room upgrade level. Old cell coordinates (0..11, 0..6) remain 100% valid within [0..15, 0..8].
  Save impact: **Zero breaking changes**. Old saves load safely without migrations.
  Files: `web/home/features/places/room-geometry.js`, `web/home/features/places/scenes.js`, `web/home/canvas-pig.js`, `assets/data/place_scenes.json:14`.

## 4. Park 384x192 -> 256x144 (Blockers, Exits, and Geometry)

Park is defined in `assets/data/place_scenes.json:47-79` as `size: [384, 192]`, `walk: [10, 112, 374, 186]`, `behindLine: 106`. Exits: `left: { "to": "garden", "x": 4 }`, `right: { "to": "farm", "x": 380 }`.

### Adjustments for 256×144:
1. `exits.right.x`: Was `380`. Must change to `252` (matching `garden` exit at 253 and `farm` at 252).
2. `walk`: Was `[10, 112, 374, 186]`. In 144 height, `Y=186` is 42 px offscreen. Must change to `[8, 80, 248, 138]` (matching `garden`).
3. `behindLine`: Was `106`. Must move to `74`.
4. Blocker pruning:
   - Out of bounds in X (>256): `tree_trunk_3` [275, 68, 291, 80], `tree_trunk_4` [330, 68, 346, 80], `bench_right` [340, 156, 370, 166], `lantern_mid` [284, 122, 290, 130], `lantern_right` [348, 178, 356, 186], `river_bank_right` [198..384].
   - Out of bounds in Y (>144): `bench_right` (Y 156-166) and `lantern_right` (Y 178-186).
5. Composition retained: Gazebo on far bank (`X=70`), 2 big trees (`X=2`, `X=212`), stream (`Y=54-68`), bridge (`X=120`), cobblestone path down, 1 bench (`X=112`), 1 lantern (`X=32`).
6. Scrolling Crop vs Fixed: A 384-wide scrolling canvas adds camera tracking complexity and breaks the instant one-screen overview shared by garden, farm, and market.
7. **Decision:** Regenerate park background as clean 256×144 in `tools/art/world/park_bg.py`. Update `place_scenes.json` size to `[256, 144]`, `walk: [8, 80, 248, 138]`, `behindLine: 74`, `exits.right.x: 252`, and prune the 6 out-of-bounds blockers.
