## 3. Risks

### 1. Overlap with P43e (Lamps on Scene)
- **The risk**: P43e (`docs/prompts/PK-P43e-room-and-garden-objects.md:25`, `docs/opt/foundations/R43.md:13, 93`) binds placed lamps to hotspots via `CanvasPig.setHotspots` and calls `toggleLamp(uid)`. If P6 also attaches canvas lamp clicks or manages hotspots, both packages will collide.
- **Resolution**:
  - P43e owns hotspot registration and the click-event trigger (`onHotspot` -> `toggleLamp`).
  - P6 owns lighting data validation: guarding against toggling non-toggleable items (`toggle: false` in `lights.js` and `toggleLamp`), removing phantom center glow, and deleting the obsolete `initCanvasLampClick` (`places/feature.js:782`).
  - P6 never calls `CanvasPig.setHotspots` for lamps.

### 2. Overlap with P46 (Outdoor Movable Instances)
- **The risk**: P46 (`docs/opt/foundations/R46.md:95-103`) turns outdoor static scenery into movable instances in `world.Rooms[place].Items`. PK-P46c modifies `web/home/features/places/edit-panel.js` for fixture locking (`fixture_locked`). If P6 edits `edit-panel.js` concurrently, merge conflicts occur.
- **Resolution**:
  - Strict wave sequencing: P46c runs first in Fala 2; P6 stash drawer enhancements (`PK-P6a`) run after P46c.
  - Category filters in P6 must include P46 outdoor items (`bench_wood`, `flowerbed_tulip`, `tree_small`, `gazebo`) under `Plants` / `Outdoor`.

### 3. Scope of P6-07 (Scene Lighting & Shadows - "do dopracowania")
- **The risk**: P6-07 was marked "do dopracowania" (`docs/PLAN_POPRAWEK_UX.md:347`). Dynamic lighting and shadows could easily blow up into complex normal mapping or raymarching.
- **Proposed small scope**:
  1. *Eliminate false spots*: Remove hardcoded `(120, 65)` fallback in `places/feature.js:138`. At night (`period === 'night'`), reduce window beam intensity to 0.15 (soft moonlight).
  2. *Furniture contact shadows*: In `CanvasPig.prototype.drawThing` (`canvas-pig.js:1313`), draw a 10-line canvas ellipse under standing items (`!thing.isFlat && !thing.isWall`) at `(thing.x, thing.y)` with 22% black alpha tinted by ambient light.
  3. No new sprite assets needed; fully satisfies visual smoke check.

### Owner Decisions (max 5)

1. **Category Taxonomy in Drawer**:
   - *Default decision*: Use 5 standard tabs: `All`, `Furniture`, `Lights`, `Plants`, `Decor`, mapped by item `category` or prefix (`lamp_`, `plant_`, `flower_`, `tree_`).

2. **Room Template Application Scope**:
   - *Default decision*: Apply only wallpaper and floor skins via existing `room_skin` message, preserving placed furniture.

3. **Lamp Hotspot Interaction Authority**:
   - *Default decision*: Delegate click detection wholly to P43e (`onHotspot` -> `toggleLamp`). P6c deletes redundant `initCanvasLampClick`.

4. **Furniture Contact Shadow Rendering**:
   - *Default decision*: Procedural canvas oval `ctx.ellipse` in `canvas-pig.js:drawThing` with 22% black alpha tinted by ambient light. Zero artist dependencies.

5. **Walking Area Width in Salon**:
   - *Default decision*: Expand `room.walk` to `[8, 40, 244, 110]`. At 244 px, the pig sprite stays fully visible within the 256 px viewport without clipping.
