## Facts already found
- Coloring: `.play-level` handler (feature.js ~line 292) loads packs with `await this.loadPack(...)` and calls `this.onOpenEditor(pack, pic, nextLevel)`. Report: the click shows nothing, but after leaving More and entering Coloring again the editor/panel is there, so the first click probably runs against a stale or not yet assigned `this.onOpenEditor`/container, or `loadPack` fails silently (fetch of a pack JSON that is not served in mock mode, or a thrown error swallowed by the `async` handler).
- Vet: `#btn-vet-checkup` sends `{type:'vet_checkup'}`. `VetFeature.HandleMessage` pays `CheckupPrice`, calls `pet.Checkup()` (which only sets `Known = true` on active conditions and stores `LastCheckupUtc`) and pushes state. When the pig has no conditions NOTHING visible changes, and the clovers may even drop with no feedback. The vet panel never shows the result of the check-up. Also the message has no entry in a validation rule set (check `src/Cuy.Core/Input/Validation/` for a Vet rule set; if `vet_checkup` is unknown to `MessageValidator`, the C# handler may be skipped by the caller: confirm by reading how `Features` dispatch uses `MessageValidator`).
