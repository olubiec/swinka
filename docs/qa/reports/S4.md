# S4 – economy (D4): first purchase, before and after

Result: **target not met, no price changed.** The first-purchase milestone that the 51–90 day baseline measures (first pig-room upgrade, L2 = 400) is at day 72 for a regular player and 99 for a casual one. The 26 new items are not on that path, and the approved K3 price rule for them breaks approved pacing invariants. Reaching 14–21 days needs a structural decision (order and cost of the structural purchases, or income), which is outside this wave's scope.

## Metrics

- **A. Economy simulator, first room upgrade** (`tools/qa/economy-sim.js --variant pigrooms --runs 10`, the metric behind the D4 baseline "51–90"). Days: casual / regular / heavy.
- **B. First place (garden, 400)**, same simulator. The first structural purchase the simulator makes.
- **C. Cheapest-first model** (own script, see Assumptions). The first 400-clover purchase, and the first affordable item among the cheapest decor, with the 26 new items at their current prices.

## Timeline (typical player, cheapest-first model, before = after)

- Day 1: 30 starting clovers + gift 10 + care 20 + poops, games, coloring + first-action achievements (about 200 clovers). First decor item (15) affordable.
- Day 4: 400 clovers reached (garden or room L2 affordable).
- Without achievements: day 7. Low activity (60 min a day, 1 game, small pictures, food 25 a day, no achievements): day 10.

The simulator (A and B) is slower because it buys structural items in a fixed order: garden, farm, packs, bedroom, house L2, pack2, then the first room. That order, not the price of the room, sets the day-72 number.

## Assumptions

- Income rules from `src/Cuy.Core` (`assets/data/economy.json` defaults): starting 30; daily gift 10 + 5 per streak day, max 50, streak resets after a missed day; care tasks 5 each, max 6 a day; poops 1 clover each, spawn every 40–90 game minutes (mean 65); minigames score/10, max 40 a game, 10 rewarded games and 150 a day; coloring cells/40 clamped 5–25, 5 pictures a day.
- Typical day (cheapest-first model): plays every day; 180 minutes open (game minutes = real minutes); all four daily care tasks plus the weekly ones on their weekday; 3 minigames of score 60; 1 picture of 240 cells; food 12 clovers a day (assumption, not from code; the economy.json comment says 30–40 a day); early achievements on days 1–14 (timing assumed).
- Simulator (A, B): its own profiles (`casual`, `regular`, `heavy`; income 94 / 144 / 258 a day with randomness), 30% of income to a decor wallet, 20% to other shops, 10 runs.
- "First purchase" is undefined in the owner's notes. A and B are the two readings. C shows how the answer changes with the reading.
