Question: Best practices for daily login rewards and streaks in casual games: cycle length, what happens on a missed day (reset vs grace day), size of day-7 reward vs day-1, avoiding guilt/FOMO. Sources: game design articles, GDC, r/gamedesign.

## Findings

Mostly opinion pieces and forum threads; few hard numbers. I found no GDC talk or r/gamedesign thread I could open on this.

**Cycle length**
- 7-day cycles are the common pattern: small prizes early, a rare item on the last day. Source: game-wisdom article (blog): https://game-wisdom.com/?p=32285
- Streak milestones suggested at 3, 7, 30, 100+ days; avoid a 30-day first milestone because new users may drop out before reaching it. Source: appstorys blog (vendor blog, opinion): https://appstorys.com/blog-Streaks-Milestones-Habit-Gamification
- Duolingo landmark days are 7, 30, 100, 365 (fan-analysis site; its numbers are unsourced and the page itself notes this): https://duolingo.deconstructoroffun.com/mechanics/streaks

**Missed day: reset vs grace**
- Forum thread on gamedev.net lists the options: reset to day 1 (some say it keeps incentive, others that it disadvantages players with less time), continue from where you left off (milestone days can be lost), cycle regardless of skipped days, or a hybrid where reward type decides (rising gold keeps paying the cap after day 7, rare items reset). Source: https://gamedev.net/forums/topic/699182-daily-bonus-logic/5405685/ (forum)
- Grace tools named by blogs: limited streak freezes per month, a few extra hours past midnight, or reducing the streak count instead of resetting to zero. No numbers given. Source: https://appstorys.com/blog-Streaks-Milestones-Habit-Gamification
- Duolingo (per a fan-analysis page, unverified) caps freezes at 2 for free users and silently applies them; freezes are granted at milestones. Source: https://duolingo.deconstructoroffun.com/mechanics/streaks

**Day-7 vs day-1 size**
- Only concrete examples: 100 gold on day 1 rising to 700 on day 7 (a forum example, 7x), and a rare/legendary on day 7 in a 7-day cycle. Source: gamedev.net thread above. No source gives a recommended ratio. The size of the day-7 reward is a design choice (UNVERIFIED as a best practice).

**Guilt / FOMO**
- Countdowns and streak meters create urgency; missing a day can feel like failure even for a minor reward and can push players to quit. Source: https://game-wisdom.com/?p=32285
- Harsh resets can make users abandon rather than restart; protection features keep users who missed a day for legitimate reasons. Source: https://appstorys.com/blog-Streaks-Milestones-Habit-Gamification
- Streaks can start to "take over our decisions"; recovery tools and tying the streak to something the user values reduce the pressure (the page marks these as its own inferences). Source: https://www.jom.media/streaks/
- Login bonuses can lift day-2 return but hurt long-term retention if they reward showing up rather than playing; the author suggests persistent offline state (a crop growing) and judging by D7/D30, not return rate. Source: https://rowatcher.com/news/daily-login-bonuses-are-lazy-design-here-s-what-works-instead (opinion piece)
- Claimed lifts (20-40% DAU, up to 30% engagement) are unsourced on their pages; do not rely on them.

## Answer for Cuy
Use a 7-day cycle with a mild escalation, and never reset the whole progress: on a missed day, continue or step back a little rather than going to zero (all sources that discuss guilt warn against harsh resets).
Give a small grace tool (e.g. one free missed day per cycle, or the reward simply waits) and no countdowns or red "streak lost" messages.
Day-7 size is your choice; the only documented example is about 7x day 1 (UNVERIFIED as advice). Keep the pet's wellbeing separate from the reward so a missed day feels harmless.
