## Answer for Cuy
tracery-core (Apache-2.0, .NET Standard 1.3+) is the best candidate if you want a ready library, but it has only 4 modifiers and no stated action support; Tracery.Net (Apache-2.0) claims actions and 7 modifiers but states no framework.
None shows a visible last-update date or a release, so treat all as not clearly maintained (UNVERIFIED).
You already have a tested C# 7.3 engine, so keep it unless you need a specific feature.
