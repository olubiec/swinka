## Answer for Cuy
Use ourcade/infinite-jumper-template-phaser3 (MIT, Phaser 3.55.2) as a pattern or copy with its MIT notice.
Pattern: static platform group recycled when `y >= scrollY + 700` (moved above the camera by 50-100 px), `startFollow` with a wide deadzone, `horizontalWrap(sprite)` on x, `findBottomMostPlatform()` and game over when `player.y > bottomPlatform.y + 200`.
