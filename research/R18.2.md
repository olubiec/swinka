R18.2 Licence inventory automation.

## Answer for Cuy
nuget-license can list transitive NuGet packages and fail CI when a licence is not on its allowed list, but it does not compare against THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
A "missing from notices" check needs a short own comparison script over its JSON output (design not verified).
