Question: Recommended home health routine for pet guinea pigs: how often to weigh, what weight loss (in % or grams over what time) should trigger a vet visit, nail trimming interval, how often a routine vet check, cage cleaning frequency, fresh water and hay guidance. Veterinary sources only.

## Findings

- Merck Veterinary Manual, routine care (https://www.merckvetmanual.com/all-other-pets/guinea-pigs/routine-care-of-guinea-pigs):
  - Nail trimming: every few weeks, to prevent overgrowth.
  - Routine vet check: at least once a year, with a vet experienced in guinea pigs.
  - Cage cleaning: spot-clean daily, thorough clean weekly.
  - Hay and greens: unlimited grass hay and fresh leafy greens every day; fresh water available daily.
  - Weight: the page lists "loss of appetite or weight" as a warning sign and says to see a vet promptly. It gives NO percentage or gram threshold.
- How often to weigh: NOT FOUND in the Merck page.
- Weight loss threshold in % or grams over time: NOT FOUND in any veterinary source opened.

## Answer for Cuy
Veterinary guidance: trim nails every few weeks, do a vet check at least yearly, spot-clean daily and deep-clean weekly, and give unlimited hay, fresh greens and water daily (Merck).
Any loss of appetite or weight should prompt a prompt vet visit, but no numeric threshold is given by the source.
Do not hard-code a % or gram trigger in the app until a vet source states one.
