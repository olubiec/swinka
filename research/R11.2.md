## Answer for Cuy
All eleven members exist in 2.2.1 as listed; `GetProcessID` and `IsSystemSoundsSession` are properties (no parentheses), `Sessions` is a snapshot, so call `RefreshSessions()` each poll.
`AudioSessionState` lives in `NAudio.CoreAudioApi.Interfaces` (extra `using`).
Check `AudioMeterInformation` for null and wrap reads in try/catch (HRESULT exceptions).
Dispose `MMDevice`/`MMDeviceEnumerator`; do not `using` the `AudioSessionManager`.
