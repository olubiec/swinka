Question: Guinea pig life stages with ages (baby, juvenile, adult, senior), typical lifespan, and the list of foods that are toxic or unsafe (onion, chocolate, others) with reasons. Veterinary sources only.

## Findings

- Lifespan (PetMD, https://www.petmd.com/exotic/how-long-do-guinea-pigs-live-average-lifespan — not a veterinary-only source, flagged): hairless about 7–8 years on average; long-haired about 4 years on average; record up to 14 years.
- Life stages (same PetMD page): newborn birth–1 month; adolescent 1–6 months; adult 6 months–4 years; senior 4 years and up (senior diets and more frequent vet checks recommended).
- Toxic/unsafe foods: NOT FOUND in the sources I could open.
  - Merck nutrition page (https://www.merckvetmanual.com/all-other-pets/guinea-pigs/nutritional-problems-of-guinea-pigs): does not list foods to avoid; it names no onion or chocolate.
  - PetMD page: gives only cautions about iceberg lettuce (little nutritional value) and fruit (high sugar, occasional treat). Onion and chocolate are not mentioned.
- Onion and chocolate as toxic: NOT VERIFIED from a veterinary source here. Search results from pet sites (pangovet, thevetdesk, hepper) exist but were not opened.

## Answer for Cuy
Life stages per PetMD: newborn 0–1 month, adolescent 1–6 months, adult 6 months–4 years, senior 4+ years; average lifespan about 4 years (long-haired) to 7–8 years (hairless).
The toxic-food list (onion, chocolate) is NOT verified: the Merck and PetMD pages say nothing about them.
Do not put onion or chocolate in the game's unsafe list until a veterinary source confirms it.
