Question: Azure Artifact Signing (formerly Azure Trusted Signing) in 2026: can an individual developer (not a company)
from Poland/EU use it? Monthly price, identity validation steps, how long validation takes, and whether signed apps
get immediate SmartScreen reputation. Official Microsoft Learn links only, plus one forum report if available.

## Findings

**Individual from Poland/EU** (source: Microsoft Learn quickstart)
- Public Trust certificates: organizations in the US, Canada, EU, UK, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Switzerland, Norway, Israel.
- Individual developers must be located in the United States or Canada. Private Trust has no geographic limit.
- https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/artifact-signing/quickstart
- So a private person in Poland cannot get Public Trust (the type needed for SmartScreen/Windows trust). Poland Central is only one of the 15 Azure regions where an account can be created; it does not change eligibility.
- Q&A thread (Irish sole trader, 2026): the answer, labelled AI-generated and possibly wrong, says a non-incorporated sole trader would probably count as an individual and not qualify; no accepted answer. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5915697/can-an-irish-sole-trader-(non-incorporated)-qualif

**Price**
- Basic $9.99/month, up to 5,000 signatures, extra $0.005 each; Premium $99.99/month, up to 100,000 signatures. https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/products/artifact-signing
- Not pro-rated; free, trial and sponsored Azure subscriptions are not supported (pay-as-you-go or enterprise needed). https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/trusted-signing/faq

**Identity validation steps**
- Needs Entra tenant, Azure subscription, `Microsoft.CodeSigning` provider registered, role "Artifact Signing Identity Verifier"; done in the portal only. https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/artifact-signing/quickstart
- Individual: legal name and address come from the Azure billing account, whose type must be Individual and match a government ID. FAQ: uses Microsoft Authenticator and Verified ID with a government ID that shows an address. (quickstart, FAQ)
- Organization: name, website, emails, business identifier, address, representative name as on ID.

**How long**
- Public validation: 1 to 20 business days, longer if more documents are requested; documents issued within the last 12 months. Email link expires after 7 days (new request needed). (quickstart, FAQ)

**SmartScreen reputation**
- FAQ: reputation is not immediate; it builds and is hash-based, so a new build may still prompt. https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/trusted-signing/faq
- Microsoft: files signed with the same trusted certificate can benefit from the certificate's reputation, but a first download may still show an "unrecognized app" prompt with the publisher name. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/package-and-deploy/smartscreen-reputation
- Note: Velopack's docs claim "built-in reputation" for Artifact Signing; that is weaker evidence than the two Microsoft pages above. https://docs.velopack.io/packaging/signing

**Forum reports**
- EU organization (Estonian OÜ, account in Poland Central), 2026-06-30: validation rejected asking for documents dated in the last 12 months; blade blank, no upload field; moderator hint "do not use free host domains in email"; poster gave up. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5934347/organization-identity-validation-blade-is-blank-de
- Individual onboarding was paused in April 2025; Oct 2025 moderator: working on other countries, no ETA. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/2243504/trusted-signing-for-other-countries

## Answer for Cuy
No: individual developers must be in the US or Canada; in the EU only organizations qualify (Public Trust), so a private person in Poland is out.
Price is $9.99/month (Basic) plus identity validation of 1-20 business days.
Even signed, SmartScreen reputation is not instant; EU organization applicants report problems.
