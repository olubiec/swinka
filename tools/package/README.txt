Cuy - your desktop guinea pig
=============================

Start
1. Unzip the whole folder first (right-click the zip > Extract All).
   Keep it somewhere permanent, for example Documents\Cuy.
2. Double-click Cuy.exe.
3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
   Cuy is a small hand-made app without a paid certificate, so Windows does not know it yet.
   If Smart App Control (Windows 11) is switched on, the file may be blocked completely without a "Run anyway" button;
   either turn Smart App Control off in Windows Security or wait until Microsoft recognises the file.
4. Watch the bottom-left corner of the screen and be patient... someone shy is coming.
   Then give your guinea pig a name. It lives on your taskbar.
   Hold the mouse over it to see how it feels, click it to pet it.
   Click the little head icon next to the clock to open or close its Home.

Features in Home
- Diary records daily moments and milestones automatically every evening.
- Behaviour Album collects stickers for every cute action your pig performs.
- Birthday celebrations occur with treats, music, and popcorn jumping every year.
- Bond & Tricks grow together: unlock and perform Spin, Stand Up, Popcorn, and Hurdle Jump.
- Achievements reward clovers across care, growth, social bonding, and playtime.
- Minigames (Veggie Rain, Veggie Memory, Hay Maze) let you play and earn daily clovers.
- Care calendar reminders keep daily routines easy (Done / Later).
- Vet clinic lets you diagnose ailments and administer medicines.
- Colouring book offers retro pixel-art guinea pig coloring pages.
- Shop and decorations provide toys, houses and treats for your pet.
- Daily gift rewards you with clovers every single day.
- Settings include master volume, quiet hours, muting during fullscreen, and pig size.
- Multi-monitor choice lets you decide which display your pig walks on.
- Start a new pig anytime from Settings (your old pig save is safely backed up).

Fullscreen and Desktop Behaviour
- When a game or movie goes fullscreen, Cuy quietly stays out of your way and mutes sound effects.

Requirements
Cuy requires the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime. Windows 11 has it built-in, most Windows 10 PCs have it already.
If missing, download the "Evergreen" installer at https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/

Start with Windows
Cuy starts with Windows by default. Right-click the head icon next to the clock
and untick "Start with Windows" to turn it off.

Updates
Unzip the new version into the same folder and replace the files.
Your guinea pig is saved separately (%AppData%\Cuy) and stays.

Uninstall
Untick "Start with Windows", choose Quit, then delete this folder and %AppData%\Cuy.

Something wrong?
Send a screenshot and the file %LocalAppData%\Cuy\logs\cuy.log.
(Paste the path into the address bar of File Explorer.)
